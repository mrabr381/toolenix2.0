Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2025

1. Overview

Toolenix ("we", "our", "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect, why we collect it, and how it is handled.

2. File Processing

The majority of Toolenix tools process your files entirely within your browser using client-side JavaScript. This means your files are never transmitted to our servers. They remain on your device throughout processing and are discarded from memory when you close the page or reset the tool.

A small number of tools may load third-party libraries (such as pdf-lib or Tesseract.js) from CDN providers. These libraries execute in your browser — your file content is not sent to the CDN provider.

3. Information We Collect

We collect minimal information necessary to operate the site:

  • Standard server access logs (IP address, browser type, pages visited, timestamps) — retained for up to 30 days for security and debugging purposes.
  • Anonymised analytics data (page views, tool usage counts) to understand which tools are most useful. This data cannot identify individual users.
  • Email addresses submitted via the newsletter or contact form — used only to respond to your message or send the requested newsletter.

4. Cookies

Toolenix uses a single localStorage key (toolenix-theme) to remember your dark/light mode preference. This is stored locally in your browser and is not a tracking cookie. We do not use advertising cookies or cross-site tracking.

If we add third-party analytics or advertising, this policy will be updated accordingly.

5. Third-Party Services

We use Google Fonts to load typography. Google Fonts CDN requests may log your IP address per Google's own privacy policy. All other external dependencies are loaded from public CDNs (jsDelivr, unpkg) which may log access requests.

6. Data Retention

We do not store your files. File processing happens in your browser and no file content is retained by us after you close or reset a tool. Contact form submissions are retained for up to 12 months then deleted.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of any personal data we hold about you. Contact us at the address below to exercise these rights.

8. Children's Privacy

Toolenix is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be noted with an updated "last updated" date at the top of this page.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy? Reach us via the contact page.
