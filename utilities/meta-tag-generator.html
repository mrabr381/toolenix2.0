Why meta tags matter for SEO

The title tag is the most important on-page SEO element. It appears in browser tabs, search result headlines, and social media share previews. The meta description doesn't directly affect rankings but strongly influences click-through rate — it's your page's pitch in the search results.

Open Graph and Twitter Card

Without these tags, social platforms generate their own link previews — often with wrong images, truncated titles, or the wrong description. Open Graph controls Facebook, LinkedIn, WhatsApp, Telegram, and most other platforms. Twitter Card controls X (Twitter). Both should always be set explicitly.

Optimal dimensions and lengths

  • Title — 50–60 characters. Google's search result title truncates around 600px display width, which corresponds to roughly 60 characters for average text.
  • Meta description — 150–160 characters. Longer descriptions are truncated with ellipsis in search results.
  • OG image — 1200×630px. This is the recommended size for most platforms. Minimum is 600×315px — anything smaller renders as a small inline image rather than a large card.

Robots meta tag

index, follow (the default) tells search engines to index this page and follow links on it. noindex prevents the page from appearing in search results but doesn't block crawling. Use noindex, nofollow for admin pages, thank-you pages, and any page you don't want in search results.
