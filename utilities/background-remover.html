When flood-fill background removal works well

  • Product photos on white or solid studio backgrounds
  • Simple logos on uniform backgrounds
  • Screenshots with solid background colours
  • Images where the subject and background have clearly distinct colours

Tolerance guide

The tolerance setting controls how much colour variation is included in the background mask. Start at 30. If the background isn't fully removed, increase it. If too much of the subject is removed, decrease it. For gradients or shadows near the background, try 50–70.

For complex backgrounds

Hair, fur, transparent objects, and backgrounds with similar colours to the subject require AI-based segmentation models. Free options include: Remove.bg (limited free uses), Canva's background remover (requires account), and Adobe Express.
