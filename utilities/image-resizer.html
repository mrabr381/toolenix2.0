Pixels vs percentage resizing

  • Pixel dimensions — exact control. Useful when a platform requires a specific resolution (e.g. 1200×630 for Open Graph images, 1080×1080 for Instagram, 800×200 for email banners).
  • Percentage scaling — proportional reduction or enlargement. 50% halves the image in both dimensions. 200% doubles it.

Common image size requirements

  • Open Graph / Facebook share image — 1200×630px
  • Twitter / X card image — 1200×628px
  • Instagram post — 1080×1080px (square), 1080×1350px (portrait)
  • YouTube thumbnail — 1280×720px
  • LinkedIn banner — 1584×396px

Quality note
