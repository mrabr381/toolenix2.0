What can a QR code contain?

  • URL — links directly to any website. The most common use case. Keep URLs short to reduce QR complexity.
  • Email — opens the user's mail client pre-filled with address and subject when scanned.
  • Phone — opens the device dialler with a number pre-entered.
  • WiFi — lets guests connect to your network by scanning, without typing a password. Supported on iOS 11+ and Android 10+.
  • Plain text — any text string displayed on screen after scanning.

QR code best practices

  • Print at minimum 2×2cm for reliable scanning from arm's length
  • High contrast matters — dark QR on white background, not on gradients
  • Test your QR code with at least two devices before printing at scale
  • For URLs, use a URL shortener to reduce character count and QR complexity, which improves scan speed
  • Add a call-to-action near the QR code — most people won't scan if they don't know what they're scanning
