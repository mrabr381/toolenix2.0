Character limit reference

  • X (Twitter) — 280 characters per post
  • Instagram caption — 2,200 characters (first 125 shown before "more")
  • Meta description — 150–160 characters recommended for SEO
  • Meta title — 50–60 characters recommended
  • LinkedIn post — 3,000 characters (first 210 shown before "see more")
  • Google Business description — 750 characters

Reading time calculation

Reading time is calculated based on the average adult reading speed of 200 words per minute. This is deliberately conservative — technical content or dense prose is read more slowly. Marketing research suggests web readers scan rather than read, so shorter is often more effective regardless of the technical word count.

Word count targets for SEO

Word count correlates weakly with rankings — Google has explicitly said it's not a direct ranking factor. What matters is whether the content covers the topic thoroughly enough to satisfy the search intent. For most informational topics, 1,000–2,000 words is sufficient. Longer is only better if the additional content adds value.
