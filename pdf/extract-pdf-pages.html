Extract vs Split — what's the difference?

Split divides a PDF into multiple files. Extract creates a single new PDF containing only the pages you specify. Use Extract when you want to isolate a few pages (e.g. pages 4-7) from a 50-page document without creating many separate files.

Enter page numbers as a comma-separated list or ranges: 2, 5, 8-12
