What is a PDF merger?

A PDF merger is a tool that combines two or more separate PDF files into a single, unified document. Instead of sharing a dozen individual files, you send one clean PDF. Instead of printing multiple documents, you print one. Merging PDFs is one of the most common document tasks in any workflow — and Toolenix makes it free and instant.

Common use cases

Merging PDFs is useful across virtually every industry. Legal teams combine contracts and exhibits into one submission package. Students merge notes, readings, and assignments. Finance teams consolidate monthly reports into quarterly summaries. HR departments bundle policy documents into a single employee handbook. Photographers merge photo books. The list is endless — if you have multiple PDFs that belong together, this tool handles it.

Is it safe to merge PDFs online?

With Toolenix, yes. Unlike many online PDF tools, our merger runs entirely inside your browser using the pdf-lib JavaScript library. Your files are loaded into your browser's memory and processed there — no data is transmitted to any server. Once you download your merged file and close the page, nothing persists anywhere.

Privacy note: Toolenix does not store, log, or transmit your PDF files. Processing is 100% client-side. For highly sensitive documents, this architecture is safer than cloud-based alternatives.
