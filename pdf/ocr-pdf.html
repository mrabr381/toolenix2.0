What is OCR and when do you need it?

Scanned PDFs are images — there's no selectable text layer. OCR (Optical Character Recognition) analyses each page image and identifies characters, reconstructing the text. Once extracted, you can copy, search, and reuse the content.

Accuracy expectations

  • Clean, high-resolution scans (300 DPI+) with standard fonts: 95%+ accuracy
  • Low-resolution or skewed scans: accuracy drops significantly
  • Handwriting: Tesseract.js is not designed for handwriting recognition
  • Non-Latin scripts: accuracy varies by language — English is most reliable

This tool extracts text from the first page for preview. For multi-page PDFs with full searchable PDF output, see our Searchable PDF tool.
