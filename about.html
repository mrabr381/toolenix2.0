Our Mission

Free tools for everyone

We built Toolenix because the best tools online are either paywalled, ad-stuffed, or demand your email before letting you do anything. That's backwards.

Every tool on Toolenix runs entirely in your browser where possible. Your files don't touch our servers. We make money through ads — not by monetizing your data.
